# 🌾 LR AgroSense - Smart Farming • AI • IoT

> **Empowering Farmers Through Affordable AI & IoT Solutions**

LR AgroSense is an early-stage Agritech startup dedicated to transforming agriculture through accessible AI and IoT solutions. Our mission is to help farmers improve productivity, reduce crop losses, optimize water and fertilizer usage, and promote sustainable farming practices.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Real-Time Soil Telemetry**: Monitor Soil Moisture, Soil pH, NPK levels, Temperature, and Humidity.
- **AI Crop Advisory**: Automated machine learning insights to optimize crop yield and prevent soil degradation.
- **Local Language Voice Alerts**: Spoken audio broadcasts in Telugu, Hindi, English, Tamil, and Kannada for regional farmers.
- **Mobile Responsive**: Fully responsive investor-ready interface built with React 18, Vite, and Tailwind CSS.
- **Student Internship & Research Network**: Platform for students interested in Agritech, Agriculture Science, and Startup Building.
