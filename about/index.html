About LR AgroSense

LR AgroSense was founded in 2026 by Pampana Hari Sai Ganesh to bridge the gap between advanced agricultural hardware engineering and smallholder farming in India.

Founder & CEO: Pampana Hari Sai Ganesh

Pampana Hari Sai Ganesh leads LR AgroSense with a vision to eliminate agricultural input waste, preserve regional water tables, and deliver actionable micro-climate intelligence directly to farmers.
