ABOUT LR AGROSENSE

Transforming Agricultural Ecosystems with Technology

LR AgroSense is an early-stage AgriTech startup committed to democratizing modern precision farming. By integrating robust Internet of Things (IoT) sensors, automated irrigation controllers, and AgriMind AI models, we empower farmers to monitor soil moisture, NPK ratios, and water pH levels in real time — reducing input costs while expanding crop yields.

👁️

Our Vision

To build India's most accessible, reliable, and intelligent precision farming platform — establishing data-driven crop management standard operating procedures across every acre of farmland.

🎯

Our Mission

Engineered to design micro-cost hardware sensors and conversational AI systems that allow farmers to eliminate guesswork, mitigate pest outbreaks, and optimize fertilizer application with pin-point accuracy.

How LR AgroSense Works

Deploy Field Sensor

02

Real-Time Telemetry

Sensors stream continuous soil moisture, NPK, and water pH readings via GSM/LoRaWAN.

AI engine processes telemetry against satellite data and regional ICAR packages of practice.

Farmers receive exact irrigation schedules and fertilizer dosages on their smartphones.
