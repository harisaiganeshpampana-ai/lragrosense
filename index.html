ABOUT LR AGROSENSE

Transforming Agricultural Ecosystems with Technology

LR AgroSense is an early-stage AgriTech startup committed to democratizing precision farming. By integrating IoT sensors and automated irrigation controllers, we empower farmers to monitor soil moisture, NPK ratios, and water pH levels in real time.

👁️

Our Vision

To build India's most accessible and intelligent precision farming platform — establishing data-driven crop management across every acre of farmland.

🎯

Our Mission

Design micro-cost hardware sensors that allow farmers to eliminate guesswork, mitigate pest outbreaks, and optimize fertilizer application with accuracy.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026 • LR AgroSense

1. Information We Collect

When you apply for an internship or interact with LR AgroSense, we collect personal details supplied voluntarily by you, including:

  • Applicant Profile: Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number.
  • Academic Background: College / University Name, Degree / Major, Graduation Year.
  • Application Documents: Resume / CV (uploaded PDF/Word file or Google Drive / Dropbox URL), LinkedIn Profile URL, and Portfolio / GitHub URL.
  • Motivation & Availability: Why you wish to join LR AgroSense, preferred start date, and availability.
2. Purpose of Collection

We collect and process your information strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • Evaluating candidate qualifications for remote internship opportunities.
  • Contacting applicants for screening interviews and onboarding communications.
  • Managing LR AgroSense's internal Applicant Tracking System (ATS).
3. Data Access & Strict Confidentiality

Your applicant data is strictly confidential. Only Founder & CEO Pampana Hari Sai Ganesh has access to applicant records.

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, lease, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers, recruiters, data brokers, or marketing partners.

4. Secure Storage & Infrastructure

All data is transmitted securely over SSL/TLS encrypted connections and stored within Google Cloud Firebase / Firestore infrastructure protected by Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We retain candidate records for up to 12 months for active and future recruitment considerations. Applicants may request immediate and complete deletion of their profile at any time by emailing us.

6. Cookies & Analytics

We use standard browser local storage (localStorage) to maintain session state and basic Firebase web analytics to monitor website uptime. We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies.

7. Privacy Contact & Inquiries

For privacy queries, data access, or deletion requests, contact Founder Pampana Hari Sai Ganesh directly:
