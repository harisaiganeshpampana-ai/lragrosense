ABOUT LR AGROSENSE

Transforming Agricultural Ecosystems with Technology

LR AgroSense is an early-stage AgriTech startup committed to democratizing precision farming. By integrating IoT sensors and automated irrigation controllers, we empower farmers to monitor soil moisture, NPK ratios, and water pH levels in real time.

👁️

Our Vision

To build India's most accessible and intelligent precision farming platform — establishing data-driven crop management across every acre of farmland.

🎯

Our Mission

Design micro-cost hardware sensors that allow farmers to eliminate guesswork, mitigate pest outbreaks, and optimize fertilizer application with accuracy.

OPENING 03

IoT & Electronics Intern

Department: Hardware Engineering

Job Description

Design, prototype, and test low-cost IoT sensor nodes using ESP32 microcontrollers, Modbus RS485 soil probes, and LoRaWAN communication modules for field deployment.

Responsibilities

  • Design and assemble ESP32-based sensor node prototypes
  • Interface Modbus RS485 soil NPK sensors and calibrate readings
  • Develop firmware for sensor data acquisition and wireless transmission
  • Test solar power management and battery charging circuits
  • Document hardware specifications and create assembly guides

Eligibility

  • Pursuing B.Tech/B.E. in ECE, EEE, or related engineering fields
  • Experience with Arduino, ESP32, or Raspberry Pi
  • Basic knowledge of circuit design and PCB layout
  • Familiarity with C/C++ for embedded systems

Benefits

  • LinkedIn recommendation from the Founder
  • Hands-on experience building real IoT hardware products
  • Mentorship in embedded systems and product engineering
  • Letter of Recommendation upon exceptional performance
