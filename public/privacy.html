Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

LR AgroSense is a sole proprietorship registered as an MSME (Udyam: UDYAM-AP-19-0077611), based in Eluru, Andhra Pradesh, India, and owned by Pampana Hari Sai Ganesh. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean LR AgroSense, and "you" means anyone who uses our website, portals or products.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how we keep it safe, and the choices you have. It follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000. It applies to:

  • our website, lragrosense.in
  • the intern portal, manager portal and team portal on that website
  • emails you send to or receive from any @lragrosense.in address
  • LR AgroSense devices, such as the SMS pump controller, once they are sold or piloted

2. What we collect

Who you are What we collect Why
Internship applicant Name, email, phone number, college, course, year of study, resume and anything else you put in your application To review your application and contact you
Intern Login email, a securely hashed password, daily updates, and field reports with farmer-visit photos, dates and times To run the internship, check the work and issue certificates
Manager or team member Name, work title, login email, hashed password, messages, call logs and files shared in the portal To let the team work together
Farmer met during field research First name, village, crop, the problems discussed and photos, only with the farmer's permission To understand farm problems and design our products
Customer or pilot farmer (devices) Name, phone numbers registered to control the pump, village, installation address, soil moisture and power readings, pump on/off times and SMS commands To run the device, send alerts, give support and improve the product
Anyone who emails us Your email address and what you write To reply to you

We do not collect payment card or bank details on our website. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell personal data to anyone.

3. Cookies and browser storage

Our portals keep a login token in your browser so you stay signed in. If you have turned on portal notifications, your browser shows them using its own notification settings. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Signing out, or clearing your browser data, removes the login token.

4. Consent

We collect personal data only when you give it to us for a clear purpose: when you apply, sign up through an invite, submit a report, buy or pilot a device, or email us. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us (section 9). Withdrawing does not undo what we did lawfully before, and we may no longer be able to provide the service. For example, an intern account cannot run without a login email.

Interns must ask farmers for permission before taking photos or recording their details, and must not photograph children.

5. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers who help us run LR AgroSense. They may use it only to provide their service to us.

  • Google Cloud (Cloud Run, Mumbai region): runs our portal server
  • MongoDB Atlas: stores portal accounts, reports, messages and files
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the public website
  • Zoho Mail: our company email
  • Mobile network operators: deliver SMS commands and alerts for our devices

We may also share data if the law or a government authority requires it, or to protect someone's safety. If we take part in a merger or become a private limited company, your data will move to the new company under this same policy.

We never publish a farmer's name or photo in marketing without that farmer's written permission.

6. How long we keep data

Data How long
Applications from people we did not select 12 months, then deleted
Intern accounts and reports 3 years after the internship ends, to verify certificates
Team and manager accounts, messages and files While the person is on the team, then up to 1 year
Device customer data and readings While the device is in use, then up to 2 years
Emails As long as needed to answer and keep business records

We may keep data longer when the law requires it.

7. How we keep data safe

  • Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. Nobody at LR AgroSense can see your password.
  • Portal accounts are created only by invitation, and each person sees only their own role's portal.
  • Data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Only the people who need data for their work can open it.
  • Our devices act only on SMS commands from registered phone numbers.

If a data breach is likely to affect you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it
  • correct or update data that is wrong or incomplete
  • delete your data when it is no longer needed for the purpose you gave it
  • withdraw your consent
  • name another person to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself

We aim to reply within 30 days. If you are unhappy with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Our website and portals are meant for adults. If an applicant is under 18, a parent or guardian must give consent before the person applies. We will delete a minor's data if we learn it was given without that consent.

We may update this policy as our products grow. We will post the new version on this page with a new date, and tell portal users about important changes.
